## Development and presentation scope

House modeling is working in the Gemini prototype, with phase 2 house/site/landscape refinement as the current focus. Integrated interior walkthroughs and photorealistic interior generation remain planned phase 3 work.

The interior walkthrough is a separate recording, and the interior images illustrate the intended phase 3 experience. They are presentation examples; they do not establish an integrated production workflow, source-plan accuracy or parity with the latest builds.
